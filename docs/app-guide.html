📖 App Guide

Everything you need to know to get the most out of ExcelDriving G1

Getting Started

1

Install the App

Download ExcelDriving G1 from the App Store (iOS/iPadOS) or Google Play (Android). No account required — open it and you're ready to go.

2

Choose Your Language

Tap the Settings tab, then under Appearance set Language to English or Français. All 352 questions and every UI element will switch immediately.

3

Pick a Practice Mode

From the Practice tab, choose:

  • Quick Check — 10 questions, ~5 minutes
  • Practice Test — 20 questions, ~15 minutes
  • Mock G1 Test — 40 questions, ~40 minutes (mirrors the real test)
4

Review Your Results

After each session you'll see a detailed breakdown: score, pass/fail, XP earned, and a full answer review with explanations for every question.

Smart Learning

🧠

Review Mistakes

After getting questions wrong, they appear in your Review Mistakes queue. Start a Smart session from the Practice tab to focus only on those questions until you master them.

🔖

Bookmark Questions

During any session, tap the bookmark icon on a question to save it. Access bookmarked questions any time via the Bookmarked Questions Smart session.

📉

Weak Area Focus

The app tracks your accuracy per topic. If any category drops below the threshold, it appears under Weak Area Focus so you can target exactly what needs work.

Tips for Success

🎯 Aim for 85%+ in practice before booking your real G1 test. The official passing score is 80% (32/40), so leave yourself a comfortable margin.

🔁 Use Smart Learning daily. Even 10 minutes a day of targeted review on weak areas is more effective than a single long session.

📖 Read the explanations. Every question comes with a detailed explanation. Don't just check if you're right — understand why.

🌙 Try Dark Mode. In Settings → Theme → Dark. Easier on the eyes during evening study sessions.
